      How to play

      Watch the clock and earn points when the time matches one or more rules.

      1. Keep this page open and let each minute roll over.
      2. When a rule matches, your time score increases automatically.
      3. Stack matches in the same minute to get higher scores and unlock badges.
      4. Score at least one point a day to keep your streak alive.

      Rules are judged on the 12-hour clock face, so most patterns score twice a day (AM and PM). A few special moments, like 13:37 or midnight, only happen once a day.
